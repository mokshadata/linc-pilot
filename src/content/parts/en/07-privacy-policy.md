### Privacy Policy for FortBendLINC.org

**Effective Date**: Jan 1 2026

Welcome to **fortbendlinc.org** (“we,” “us,” “our,” or “Website”). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect the personal information you provide when you visit or interact with our Website and related services. By accessing or using the Website, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

    We may collect the following types of information:

    1. Personal Information You Provide Directly

        When you use forms or applications on the Website (for example, when you apply for rental assistance), we may collect personal information such as:

        * Full name
        * Postal and email address
        * Phone number
        * Demographic information
        * Other information you voluntarily provide

    1. Automatically Collected Information

        When you browse the Website, we and our service providers may automatically collect certain information through server logs, cookies, and similar technologies (“automated data”). This may include:

        * IP address
        * Browser type and version
        * Device and operating system
        * Pages viewed and actions taken on the Website
        * Time and date of visit and session duration

2. How We Use Your Information

    We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

    * To provide, operate, maintain, and improve the Website and its services
    * To respond to your inquiries or requests
    * To administer and process applications for assistance
    * To communicate with you about program eligibility, updates, and important notices
    * To monitor and analyze usage, trends, and performance of the Website
    * To comply with legal and regulatory requirements

3. Cookies and Similar Technologies

    We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience, recognize you on return visits, and gather usage information. You may refuse cookies through your browser settings; however, some features of the Website may not function properly if you disable cookies. The Website does not currently respond to Do Not Track (DNT) signals sent by browsers.

4. How We Share Your Information

    We do **not** sell or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information in the following limited circumstances:

    * With service providers or partners who assist us in operations (e.g., web hosting, data processing) under confidentiality agreements
    * To comply with legal obligations, such as responding to a subpoena, court order, or other legal process
    * To protect and defend our rights or property, or the safety of our Website users

5. Third-Party Links and Services

    The Website may contain links to third-party websites or services (including social media and housing resources). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites. We encourage you to read the privacy policies of any third-party services you access.

6. Data Security

    We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your data from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

7. Children’s Privacy

    Our services are **not directed to children under the age of 13**, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected such information, please contact us so we can delete it.

8. Your Choices and Rights

    Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights regarding your personal information, such as access, correction, or deletion of your data. To exercise these rights, please contact us using the information below.

9. Changes to This Policy

    We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Effective Date” at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision. Your continued use of the Website after such changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact Us

    If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact:
