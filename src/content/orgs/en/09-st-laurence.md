alt: The Social Concerns Ministry's (SCM) staff is the main point of contact for individuals and other groups who are seeking support or services for a variety of needs. The staff assesses those needs and determines if they can be provided for through the Parish or if referral to other trusted organizations is needed. The ministry engages the St. Laurence community in service to others by offereing a variety of opportunities for parishioners to share their time, treasure and talents to help them accomplish their mission.
