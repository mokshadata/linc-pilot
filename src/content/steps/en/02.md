* If you are eligible to apply, you receive an invitation by email to submit a full application.
* The application takes approximately 20 minutes to complete.
* You will fill out information about
    * your household,
    * your property and landlord or utility provider, along with
    * how much assistance you are requesting.
* You will need to upload a copy of a Photo ID and a copy of your Rental Agreement or Utility Bill, based on the assistance you are asking for.
